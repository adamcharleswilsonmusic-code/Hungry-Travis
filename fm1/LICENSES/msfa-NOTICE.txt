Hungry Travis retains the FM6 lookup-table derivations in tools/gen_tables.py
from its platform lineage. They adapt msfa formulas to Python-generated
tables. The literal 100-entry FM6_LFO_SOURCE values also match the lfoSource
table in Dexed's msfa component. This package does not contain the complete
msfa or Dexed synthesizer engine.

Verified upstream source references:

- msfa LFO, Copyright 2013 Google Inc.:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/music-synthesizer-for-android/master/app/src/main/jni/lfo.cc
- msfa pitch-envelope formulas, Copyright 2012 Google Inc.:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/music-synthesizer-for-android/master/app/src/main/jni/pitchenv.cc
- msfa frequency lookup, Copyright 2012 Google Inc.:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/music-synthesizer-for-android/master/app/src/main/jni/freqlut.cc
- Dexed msfa LFO table, Copyright 2013 Google Inc.:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/asb2m10/dexed/master/source/msfa/lfo.cc
- Dexed's README states that its msfa component remains Apache-2.0:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/asb2m10/dexed/master/README.md
